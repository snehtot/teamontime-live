COMMON QUESTIONS

Ecommerce Development FAQs

Key details on ecommerce platforms, security, and checkout optimization.

Which ecommerce platform is right for our brand?

We conduct a technical discovery session to analyze your catalog size, order volume, international currency needs, back-office integrations, and internal maintenance capacity. We recommend Shopify for businesses seeking hosted reliability and rapid scaling, and custom WooCommerce for brands needing self-hosted data ownership and open-source flexibility.

Can you migrate our products, customer accounts, and order history from our existing store?

Yes. We have conducted dozens of complex migrations from Magento, OpenCart, and WooCommerce to modern platforms like Shopify. We migrate SKUs, product variants, high-resolution imagery, customer order histories, and implement comprehensive 301 redirects to protect existing search engine rankings.

How do you protect online store transactions and customer data?

Security is foundational to our engineering. We enforce Level 1 PCI-DSS compliance standards, end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, secure API token storage, CSRF protection, and regular automated dependency audits to prevent vulnerabilities.
