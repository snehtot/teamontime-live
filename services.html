COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our technical standards, collaboration process, and service capabilities.

How does Team On Time determine project costs without fixed public prices?

Because no two businesses or websites have identical technical requirements, we do not use cookie-cutter price tables. We analyze your scope—including visual design fidelity, custom functional requirements, database architecture, third-party APIs, and compliance needs—and provide a transparent, detailed proposal with itemized milestones before any development starts.

How long does a typical custom website or Shopify build take?

Timelines depend on scope and architecture. A focused bespoke business website or custom Shopify theme generally ranges from 3 to 6 weeks, while complex ecommerce migrations or custom web application portals with extensive third-party integrations typically take 6 to 12 weeks. Every engagement is paired with a clear milestone schedule.

Do you build custom themes or modify existing pre-made templates?

We specialize in custom theme engineering using semantic HTML, modern CSS, and clean Liquid/Gutenberg blocks to avoid the performance debt, bloat, and security vulnerabilities of marketplace themes. If a client specifically prefers starting from a clean framework such as Shopify Dawn, we customize and optimize it with bespoke sections and rigorous speed tuning.

What quality assurance standards are applied to our codebase?

Every line of code passes through our proprietary 42-Point Pre-Flight QA Protocol prior to release. This includes cross-browser verification, mobile responsiveness testing, Core Web Vitals validation, W3C markup hygiene, schema structured data verification, and rigorous security checks.
