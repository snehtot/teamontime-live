FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Website Development FAQs

Key details on our technology stack, pricing philosophy, and delivery standards.

How much does custom website development cost?

Project costs are tailored to the unique scope of your build. Factors include the number of custom page layouts, CMS complexity, third-party software integrations, data migration needs, and interactive features. We avoid arbitrary rate packages and provide a detailed, fixed-scope proposal after understanding your exact project requirements.

Will our new website be fully mobile responsive and accessible?

Yes, 100%. We take a mobile-first approach to frontend architecture, ensuring seamless layout reflows, touch targets, and typography scaling across all screen sizes. We also adhere to WCAG accessibility guidelines to ensure usability for all users.

Do you handle domain migration and SEO redirects from our old website?

Yes. If you are replacing an existing website, our technical SEO team crawls your old domain, creates a 1-to-1 301 redirect map for all indexable URLs, and preserves canonical meta structures so your historical search equity and traffic are protected.

Who owns the code and intellectual property upon completion?

You own 100% of the code, designs, and database assets upon project sign-off. We do not lock you into proprietary hosting or closed frameworks. You receive full Git repository access and complete documentation.
