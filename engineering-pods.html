07 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ENGINEERING PODS FAQ.

Clear answers about pod capacity allocation, Slack SLAs, QA protocols, and code ownership.

What is a dedicated white-label engineering pod? +
A dedicated engineering pod is a pre-assembled team of senior full-time software engineers allocated to your agency for 160 hours per month. Pods integrate directly into your agency Slack and Git repository, operating invisibly under your brand under bilateral NDA.
How does TeamOnTime enforce sub-15 minute Slack response SLAs? +
Each pod includes a dedicated Lead Tech Lead monitored by our TeamOnTime OS SLA engine. Pings inside your agency Slack channel are acknowledged and triaged within sub-15 minutes during standard operational sprints.
What is the 42-point QA pre-flight checklist? +
The 42-point QA protocol is a mandatory testing suite executed before any staging code is released. It covers cross-browser compatibility, mobile responsiveness, Lighthouse performance, accessibility, security linters, and regression verification.
Can we scale pod hours up or down based on client retainer flux? +
Yes. Agency partners can expand capacity by adding additional 160h/month pods or flexible monthly hours to absorb project spikes without fixed payroll liability.
How is code security and IP ownership protected? +
All intellectual property rights are automatically assigned to your agency upon commit. Code is authored inside your private repositories under bilateral NDA with zero TeamOnTime code attribution.
How can I verify these claims? +
Every pod SLA benchmark, sprint capacity metric, and 42-point QA checkpoint is transparently backed by empirical verification in our central Proof Center. You can inspect actual pod workflow benchmarks, review QA protocols, or request sanitized sprint logs under bilateral NDA.
