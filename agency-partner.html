08 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

AGENCY PARTNER FAQ

Direct answers to common operational questions from agency founders and operations leaders.

How does TeamOnTime operate as an invisible backend for agencies? +
We integrate directly into your agency workflow. Our engineers work in your agency Slack channels, commit code to your GitHub/GitLab repositories under your domain email aliases, and follow your project management conventions. There is zero client-facing TeamOnTime attribution.
What does our agency retain versus what TeamOnTime handles? +
Your agency retains 100% of the client relationship, account strategy, brand spotlight, client communication, and public credit. TeamOnTime handles the agreed technical execution, code development, QA audits, speed optimization, and staging deployments.
How does TeamOnTime protect client confidentiality and prevent poaching? +
Every engagement begins with a strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). TeamOnTime has zero direct contact with your clients, never solicits your accounts, and transfers complete repository and intellectual property ownership to your agency.
What is TeamOnTime's actual team structure? +
TeamOnTime operates a 23-specialist engineering roster: 3 Shopify developers, 5 WordPress developers, 3 React/Node.js engineers, 4 SEO/Search engineers, 2 AI systems developers, 2 QA specialists, 2 UI/UX designers, and 2 Technical Project Managers. Capacity is allocated via dedicated pods or fixed-scope sprints.
Which agency profiles are the best fit for TeamOnTime? +
The best fit is boutique and mid-market digital, web design, ecommerce, and SEO agencies with 5–25 team members that have existing sales, design, and client management capability but need reliable, scalable technical engineering execution.
What commercial engagement models are available for agencies? +
We offer dedicated monthly engineering pods (160 hours/month per pod) for continuous delivery and retainers, as well as custom-scoped milestone proposals for standalone Shopify builds, platform migrations, or technical search overhauls.
How can I verify these claims? +
Every partnership capability, pod delivery metric, and 42-point QA standard is transparently documented in our central Proof Center. You can inspect live code practices, review SLA verification methods, or request sanitized partner case studies under bilateral NDA.
