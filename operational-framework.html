08 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Clear answers about our operational workflows, SLAs, and white-label protocols.

What is included in the operational framework? +
Our operational framework includes SLA-backed delivery timelines, bilateral NDA protocols, dedicated team pods, 42-point quality assurance checkpoints, and transparent asynchronous reporting under your agency brand.
How quickly can you onboard a new agency partner? +
Most agency partners are fully onboarded within 5 business days including NDA execution, Git repository credential setup, Slack pod assignment, and first project briefing.
Do my clients interact with TeamOnTime? +
No. We operate under absolute white-label isolation. We never contact your clients, use client contact info, or claim direct credit for projects.
How are commits and Git repositories handled? +
All commits are pushed under generic developer aliases or your agency accounts. We scrub metadata logs and Git author references to ensure complete white-label privacy.
What happens in a critical emergency? +
Our emergency protocols guarantee that a Senior Systems Architect is deployed within 15 minutes of a critical alert to isolate issues and execute instant rollback recovery.
Can we adjust our resource allocation slots? +
Yes. Under our Variable Capacity Pod framework, agencies can expand or retract dedicated 160h/mo pod capacity with 30 days notice to adapt to client retainer flux.
