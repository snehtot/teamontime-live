09 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SHOPIFY WHITE-LABEL FAQ

Direct operational answers for agency founders, operations directors, and technical leads.

What is white-label Shopify development? +
White-label Shopify development is a partnership model where a dedicated technical team builds, migrates, customizes, and maintains Shopify and Shopify Plus stores on behalf of an agency. The agency retains 100% of client ownership, account management, creative direction, and billing. TeamOnTime operates strictly behind the scenes under bilateral NDA, committing code directly to the agency's Git repositories and collaborating via internal agency channels without client attribution.
When should a Shopify agency use a white-label development partner? +
A Shopify agency should engage a white-label development partner when internal engineering capacity is fully booked, project pipelines surge beyond in-house bandwidth, or seasonal delivery spikes risk client deadlines. It is also ideal when agencies win complex Shopify Plus builds requiring advanced architectures—like Checkout Extensibility, Shopify Functions, or custom private apps—without taking on the high overhead and bench risk of permanent full-time hiring.
What Shopify work can a white-label development team handle? +
A full-stack white-label engineering team handles the complete technical Shopify lifecycle. This includes custom Online Store 2.0 Liquid theme engineering from Figma designs, legacy theme refactoring, Checkout Extensibility upgrades, server-side Shopify Functions, custom app and middleware development, third-party ERP/CRM integrations, headless Hydrogen commerce builds with Storefront APIs, Core Web Vitals optimization, and ongoing monthly retainer maintenance tickets backed by multi-device QA.
Can a white-label partner work under an agency's brand? +
Yes. Complete brand invisibility is the foundation of white-label delivery. TeamOnTime developers integrate directly into the agency's toolchain using agency domain email addresses, join internal agency Slack or Teams workspaces, and commit code directly into agency GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repositories. All deliverables, pull requests, staging links, and documentation are strictly white-labeled, ensuring merchant clients only ever see the agency's brand.
Can TeamOnTime work under NDA? +
Yes. Every engagement is governed by a legally binding bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) executed before any repository access, technical scoping, or communication begins. The agreement guarantees strict confidentiality, prohibits any direct contact with agency clients, and ensures that 100% of all code, intellectual property, custom assets, and documentation belong entirely and exclusively to your agency.
How does agency-client communication work? +
Communication operates on an agency-led model. Your agency account managers and technical directors maintain direct, exclusive communication with your merchant clients. TeamOnTime operates as an invisible backend engineering department communicating directly with your internal team via Slack, Teams, ClickUp, Asana, or Jira. We attend internal agency sprint standups and technical planning sessions, but never participate in client-facing meetings unless explicitly requested under agency email aliases.
What is Shopify Online Store 2.0? +
Shopify Online Store 2.0 is Shopify's modern theme architecture that replaced legacy Liquid templates with modular JSON templates, sections everywhere, and dynamic App Blocks. It allows merchants to customize page layouts visually through the theme editor without editing code, while enabling developers to engineer clean, performant Liquid themes with decoupled CSS and JavaScript, structured metafields, and superior Core Web Vitals performance.
What is Shopify Checkout Extensibility? +
Shopify Checkout Extensibility is Shopify's app-based, sandboxed framework that replaces deprecated checkout.liquid templates and legacy script tags. It enables upgrade-safe customizations across checkout, thank-you, and order status pages using sandboxed Checkout UI Extensions (built with React and TypeScript), server-side Shopify Functions, and the Web Pixels API, ensuring fast, secure, and accelerated checkout compatibility (such as Shop Pay).
What are Shopify Functions? +
Shopify Functions are backend customizations that execute server-side on Shopify's global infrastructure, replacing legacy Ruby Shopify Scripts. Compiled to WebAssembly (Wasm), Functions execute in under 5 milliseconds to modify core commerce business logic—including dynamic cart and order discounts, custom delivery rules, shipping method renaming or suppression, payment gateway filtering, and custom checkout validation rules.
What is the difference between Shopify theme development and app development? +
Shopify theme development focuses on the storefront presentation layer, utilizing Online Store 2.0 JSON templates, Liquid markup, HTML5, CSS, and modular JavaScript to build customer-facing user interfaces. Shopify app development involves engineering custom backend software—using frameworks like Remix, Node.js, and GraphQL Admin APIs—to execute proprietary business logic, integrate external ERPs, store custom data via metaobjects, or extend checkout behavior through Shopify Functions and Checkout UI Extensions.
When should an agency use Shopify Plus? +
An agency should recommend Shopify Plus when a merchant requires advanced enterprise capabilities. Key triggers include custom Checkout Extensibility UI modifications, custom backend business logic via bespoke Shopify Functions, dedicated B2B wholesale portals with custom pricing tiers, multi-store architecture across global expansion stores, automated workflows via Shopify Flow and Launchpad, or custom headless commerce deployments leveraging Hydrogen and the Storefront API.
How does white-label Shopify development help agencies handle delivery spikes? +
White-label Shopify development provides agencies with elastic engineering capacity that expands and contracts with project demand. During seasonal peaks (such as Q3/Q4 holiday build-outs) or sudden client acquisition spikes, an agency can instantly scale up dedicated developer pods without recruitment lead times or long-term overhead. When project volume stabilizes, capacity adjusts gracefully, protecting agency gross margins and eliminating non-billable bench costs.
