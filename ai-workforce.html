08 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

AI WORKFORCE FAQ

Everything agencies need to know about deploying autonomous AI teammates.

What is an autonomous AI workforce teammate? +
An autonomous AI workforce teammate is a custom-engineered AI agent that handles specific operational workflows—such as customer support ticket ingestion, Gorgias vector RAG search, Shopify store ops webhooks, and automated reporting—operating 24/7 without manual intervention.
How does vector RAG pipeline integration work for e-commerce stores? +
Vector Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) indexes your store's product catalog, order history, and help center documentation into high-dimensional vector embeddings. When customer queries arrive, the AI retrieves relevant knowledge in real-time to deliver precise, context-aware responses.
Are TeamOnTime AI Workforce systems safe under agency NDA? +
Yes. All AI pipelines, API keys, and training vectors are kept strictly private inside enterprise tenant environments. Zero client data is used for public model training, and all code is covered under a bilateral NDA.
What AI frameworks and vector databases does TeamOnTime engineer with? +
Our AI engineers specialize in LangChain, LlamaIndex, Python 3.11, FastAPI, Pinecone, Qdrant, ChromaDB, and pgvector embeddings with isolated VPC security boundaries.
How quickly can a dedicated AI developer pod be deployed? +
Dedicated 160h/month AI developer pods can be onboarded and integrated into your agency Slack and repository within 48 to 72 hours.
How can I verify these claims? +
Every autonomous AI workflow, RAG vector pipeline latency benchmark, and security safeguard is documented with verifiable proof in our central Proof Center. You can inspect operational sandboxing architecture, audit retrieval benchmarks, or review live agent logs under bilateral NDA.
