08 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SEARCH ENGINEERING WHITE-LABEL FAQ

Direct answers to common operational questions from agency founders and search directors.

How does white-label technical SEO fulfillment work with TeamOnTime? +
Your agency owns the client relationship, account strategy, and deliverables. TeamOnTime acts as your invisible technical execution team, implementing code-level technical SEO fixes, server log diagnostics, schema markup, and speed optimizations directly inside your staging or production repositories under bilateral NDA.
What is the difference between traditional SEO and Search Engineering™? +
Traditional SEO focuses primarily on keyword research, copywriting, and link acquisition. Search Engineering™ treats search as a software engineering discipline—diagnosing crawl efficiency in server access logs, architecting nested JSON-LD knowledge graphs, fixing rendering bottlenecks, and optimizing content structures for AI answer engines (AEO/GEO).
Do you offer guaranteed keyword rankings or traffic? +
No. Ethical technical engineering does not promise guaranteed search rankings, as algorithmic ranking factors remain external. Instead, we guarantee rigorous technical code execution, clean crawl paths, valid structured data, and sub-second Core Web Vitals performance benchmarks.
Can your engineers implement technical fixes directly on client CMS platforms? +
Yes. Our engineers work across Shopify (Online Store 2.0), WordPress (Gutenberg/PHP), React, Next.js, and custom web applications, pushing verified pull requests directly to your agency repositories without requiring your in-house team to write code.
How does TeamOnTime protect agency client relationships? +
Every partnership is protected by a strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). TeamOnTime never contacts your clients, operates under unbranded or agency email credentials, and IP ownership and assignment are governed by the engagement agreement with all deliverables assigned to your agency.
What commercial retainer options exist for agencies? +
We provide monthly technical fulfillment retainers (typically starting at ₹75,000/month per agency account) as well as dedicated 160h/month search engineering pods for agencies managing multi-client retainer portfolios.
How can I verify these claims? +
Every crawl diagnostic benchmark, CWV speed recovery metric, and structured data execution standard is supported by verifiable documentation in our central Proof Center. You can inspect actual log parsing workflows, audit our delivery methods, or review anonymized enterprise search outcomes under bilateral NDA.
