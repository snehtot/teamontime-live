08 // FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Clear answers about TeamOnTime OS, white-label operations, and bilateral NDA protocols.

Is TeamOnTime OS™ software or human engineering? +
It is a hybrid operational system. TeamOnTime OS™ provides the automated protocols, CI/CD tools, 42-point QA pipelines, and telemetry governance, executed by senior dedicated engineers under strict SLA standards.
How does white-label confidentiality work under bilateral NDA? +
Everything is executed under bilateral NDA. All code commits use your agency's domain handles, pull requests are submitted inside your GitHub/GitLab org, and pings are handled in your Slack workspace. Your end clients never hear the name TeamOnTime.
What happens if our agency needs to scale capacity up or down? +
Dedicated pods provide predictable 160h/month capacity. Additional pods across Shopify, WordPress, Technical SEO, or AI can be provisioned with 30 days notice as your agency's retainer book expands.
Who owns the code and intellectual property? +
Your agency owns 100% of all code, repositories, and intellectual property. All commits are pushed directly to your agency's GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket accounts.
