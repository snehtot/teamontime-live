CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct facts about our web development process, scoping, technology, and partnerships.

How much does custom website development cost?

Every web project is custom-scoped according to exact technical requirements, design complexity, page count, and integration needs. We do not use arbitrary rate cards or low-quality templated packages. Following a detailed technical discovery session, we provide a transparent, itemized proposal with clear milestone deliverables.

Where is Team On Time located, and what is your team size?

Our engineering office is in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Founded in 2012, our in-house team consists of 18+ senior developers, UI/UX designers, and technical search engineers. We do not outsource our work to unvetted freelance marketplaces.

Do you collaborate with digital agencies under white-label agreements?

Yes. We have a dedicated agency partnership practice providing white-label engineering pods under strict bilateral NDA. We operate quietly behind agency brands with current paying clients in the UK and South Africa, while expanding to support agency partners across target markets including the USA, Australia, and the UAE, committing code via your Git repositories with zero client-facing footprint.

How does Team On Time ensure fast page speeds and Core Web Vitals compliance?

We enforce strict performance budgets from the first line of code. We avoid bloated visual builders, minify and inline critical assets, optimize image encoding with modern WebP formats, eliminate layout shifts, and apply rigorous pre-flight QA standards across core templates before launch.
