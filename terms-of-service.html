01 • SCOPE & FULFILLMENT MODELS

White-Label Engineering Execution.

TeamOnTime operates as an invisible software engineering backend under three defined engagement tiers.

Dedicated Developer Pods

160 full hours per month of senior engineering capacity allocated per pod. Engineers integrate directly into your Slack, Jira, and GitHub orgs operating under your agency domain.

Pooled Monthly Retainers

Flexible developer hours allocated across client support tickets, micro-edits, and recurring maintenance workloads with sub-15m Slack response guarantees.

Fixed-Scope SOW Builds

Milestone-based custom Shopify Liquid theme builds, WordPress Gutenberg block suites, and technical SEO refactorings delivered under locked timelines.

02 • REVISIONS & SCOPE CONTROL

Structured Execution & Change Orders.

Eliminating operational friction through transparent change management.

Sprint Revision Windows

Each milestone includes a 7-day acceptance testing window for agency review. Revisions within original SOW specifications are executed immediately without extra cost.

Formal Change Orders

Feature requests outside approved wireframes or SOW scope require a formal Change Order detailing adjusted milestone pricing, timeline shifts, or pod hour consumption.

Suspension for Non-Payment

Retainer execution and pod capacity reservation automatically pause if invoice settlements are overdue past 7 calendar days, with a 1.5% monthly late interest fee.

03 • INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROTECTION

100% Agency IP Ownership.

Your agency retains absolute ownership over code, assets, and merchant relationships.

Work-For-Hire Code Assignment

Upon full settlement of milestone or retainer invoices, 100% of custom Liquid themes, React blocks, database schemas, and codebase updates transfer exclusively to your agency.

Strict Non-Solicitation Covenant

TeamOnTime covenants that it will never solicit, market to, or accept direct engineering engagements from your end-merchants or agency clients introduced during partnership.

Confidentiality Survival

Confidentiality and trade secret protection obligations under our Master Terms survive account termination indefinitely, ensuring permanent brand protection.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, TeamOnTime's total aggregate liability for any claims arising from services shall not exceed the actual fees paid by Agency in the three (3) months preceding the event.

Third-Party Tool Disclaimer

TeamOnTime is not liable for service downtime, platform API deprecations, or outages caused by third-party platforms including Shopify, WP Engine, AWS, Vercel, or external SaaS integrations.

05 • FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Terms of Service Governance FAQ.

What is TeamOnTime's payment refund policy?
All payments made for dedicated pod capacity, monthly retainers, or SOW milestones are non-refundable. At its sole discretion, TeamOnTime may issue service credits applicable toward future TeamOnTime services.
Do unused pod hours roll over to the next month?
Dedicated pod hours (160h/mo) represent reserved senior developer allocation and do not roll over. Pooled monthly retainer hours allow up to 20% rollover into the immediately following billing cycle.
How are out-of-scope feature requests handled?
Requests exceeding approved SOW scope are estimated as a formal Change Order or absorbed into your active monthly pod allocation upon agency approval.
Who owns the IP rights for custom Shopify Liquid themes?
100% of custom Liquid themes, React Gutenberg blocks, and database scripts belong exclusively to your agency upon complete invoice settlement as work-for-hire.
