07 // LEGAL CLARITY

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Direct answers regarding legal terms, non-solicitation boundaries, and credential rotation.

Is the TeamOnTime Standard NDA mutual (bilateral)? +
Yes. Our standard NDA is 100% bilateral, protecting both your agency's proprietary client relationships, commercial terms, and codebases, as well as TeamOnTime's engineering systems and trade secrets.
Does TeamOnTime sign custom agency NDAs or redlines? +
Yes. While our standard bilateral NDA covers 99% of agency use cases, our legal team routinely reviews and executes custom agency NDAs or redlines for enterprise growth partners within 24 hours.
What is the duration of the non-solicitation covenant? +
TeamOnTime agrees to a strict 24-month non-solicitation covenant post-partnership, guaranteeing zero direct contact, direct marketing, or solicitation of your agency's end clients.
How are Git author commits and developer identities masked? +
All developer pull requests, Git commits, and code author references log strictly under your agency email domain aliases inside your GitHub or GitLab organization, ensuring complete white-label privacy.
What is the credential purge and data erasure protocol post-project? +
Within 30 days of code handoff sign-off, all temporary staging tokens, local logs, and development credentials are automatically purged from TeamOnTime vaults, with formal written Certificates of Data Erasure issued upon request.
What governing law and dispute jurisdiction applies? +
Our standard bilateral covenant is digitally executed pursuant to Section 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), governed by the laws of India with exclusive jurisdiction in competent courts in Surat, Gujarat.
